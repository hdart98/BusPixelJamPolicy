Privacy Policy

Bus Pixel Jam

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Bus Pixel Jam is a mobile game developed and published by hudigame. This policy describes information handled when you play the game or contact us.

1. Information handled by the game

  • Local game data: Progress, virtual coins, boosters, daily reward status, and preferences such as sound, music, and vibration are saved on your device. You do not need to create an account to play.
  • Gameplay and usage data: When Firebase Analytics is active, it records interactions and events such as starting, completing, or losing a level, including the level number and outcome reason. It may also collect session and app engagement information automatically.
  • Technical data: Firebase services may process app installation identifiers, device and operating system details, language, app version, and network information. Analytics may derive an approximate region from your IP address. Advertising identifiers may be processed where available, depending on platform permissions and SDK settings.
  • Support messages: If you email us, we receive your email address and information you choose to include. Please avoid sending sensitive information.

The game does not request access to your contacts, private messages, photos, microphone, or precise GPS location.

2. Why information is used

We use local data to save your progress and preferences. Usage information helps us understand level difficulty, improve the game, and investigate problems. Firebase Remote Config allows us to retrieve app configuration values and may process installation identifiers and device information for this purpose. We use support correspondence to answer your requests.

3. Service providers

The game integrates Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Remote Config. These services process information under their applicable terms and privacy documentation:

  • Firebase privacy and security information
  • Google Privacy Policy

The information processed depends on the game version, enabled services, platform, and device permissions. App stores also process downloads and related information under their own policies.

4. Advertising and purchases

The current version does not integrate an advertising network or process real-money in-app purchases. Virtual coins and items are recorded locally. If advertising or payment services are introduced, we will update this policy to describe the relevant providers and data practices.

5. Disclosure of information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by the providers identified above to deliver their services. We may also disclose information when required by applicable law or when necessary to address fraud, security incidents, or threats to people's rights and safety.

6. Storage and retention

Local progress remains on your device until you clear the game's app data or remove it, subject to any backup and restore features provided by your device or operating system. Service-provider data is retained according to the relevant service settings and policies. We retain support messages only as needed to resolve requests and meet applicable obligations. Providers may process information outside your country.

7. Your choices and requests

You can clear local game data through your device settings. Device permissions and privacy settings may provide additional controls over identifiers and data use. To request access, correction, or deletion of information we hold, email hudigame.dev@gmail.com. Available rights depend on applicable law. We may need sufficient information to verify and locate your request; we may be unable to associate analytics records with your email address.

8. Children

The game is not intended for children younger than 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age. A parent or guardian who believes a child has supplied personal information can contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate deletion steps.

9. Security and policy updates

We use reasonable safeguards for information under our control, although no storage or transmission system can guarantee complete security. We will publish policy revisions here and revise the date above when our practices change.
